#+created: [2026-06-06 Sat]

Canonical language for the org-mode GTD system implemented in =myinit.org=.
Glossary only — no implementation detail. For the as-built workflow see
[[file:gtd-workflow.org][gtd-workflow.org]]; for the decisions behind it see [[file:docs/adr/][docs/adr/]].

* Language

** Actions & states

*** Next Action
The single, concrete, physically-doable action that moves a project forward
right now. Keyword =NEXT=. A project has exactly one.
/Avoid/: task, todo (for this meaning).

*** Parked Action
A captured project action that is real but not yet the next one to do —
waiting its turn behind the current Next Action. Keyword =TODO=. Invisible to
the =n= (Next Actions) list by design; surfaced only inside its project.
/Avoid/: backlog item, later.

*** Waiting
An action handed off or blocked, pending something external (a person, a
reply, a delivery). Keyword =WAIT=.
/Avoid/: blocked, pending, delegated (as a state name).

*** Tickler
A distinct parked state for an item to revisit later, keyword =TICK=. It is not
=NEXT= and never appears in the Next-Actions (=n=) list. =SCHEDULED= is the
orthogonal mechanism giving it a resurface date. The tickler is a behaviour
(=TICK= [+ =SCHEDULED=]), not a file.
/Avoid/: reminder, snooze, deferred next action.

*** Hard Landscape
The day's genuinely date-bound commitments — only what /must/ happen on a
specific date. Carried by =DEADLINE= and timed events in =gcal.org=, never by a
wished-for =SCHEDULED=. =SCHEDULED= means "resurface on this date" (tickler),
not "do around now".
/Avoid/: calendar (overloaded), schedule.

*** Someday/Maybe
Something not committed to — no decision to act has been made. Keyword =SOME=.
/Avoid/: backlog, wishlist, later.

** Structure

*** Project
Any outcome that needs more than one action. Marked with the =:prj:= tag
(not inherited) on a heading carrying =[/]=; identified by that tag plus its
outline path. Its actions live either as a checkbox checklist (trivial
projects) or as =TODO=/=NEXT= subtree headings (default). A project with no
=NEXT=/=WAIT=/=TICK= is /stuck/.
/Avoid/: epic, goal (a goal is a higher horizon, not a project).

*** Area of Responsibility
An ongoing sphere of life with a maintenance standard, never "done" — health,
finances, research, relationships, home. Areas /generate/ projects; the review
walks them to surface new commitments. Each lives as a top-level heading in
=life.org= (the review artifact and the area vocabulary), and is stamped on
projects/actions via the =:CATEGORY:= property (one area per item, inherited
down the subtree, shown in the agenda column). An area with no active =:prj:=
is /neglected/.
/Avoid/: context (a constraint on acting, not a sphere of life), category
(=CATEGORY= is the mechanism that carries the area, not a synonym for it),
project.

*** Context
A constraint on /where/, /with what/, /with whom/, or /at what energy/ an
action can be performed — never a description of the /kind/ of work. It answers
"can I even do this here, now?". Four flavours:
- place: =@home=, =@office=, =@lab=, =@errands=, =@klusje=
- tool: =@computer=, =@phone=, =@bank=, =@email=, =@teams=
- energy: =@focus=, =@braindead=
- with-whom: =@agenda= + a person tag (see /Agenda list/)
Kind-of-work tags (=@write=, =@program=, =@read=, =@research=, =@createplot=,
=@schedule=) are /not/ contexts — they overlap each other and =@computer= and
are demoted.
/Avoid/: label, category, area (a horizon, not a context), action-type tag.

*** Agenda list
The GTD list of things to raise with a specific person or at a specific
meeting. Carried by =@agenda= + a =:PersonName:= tag. A with-whom context: you
can only act on it when you are with that person. (Whether the per-person tags
stay is an open question.)
/Avoid/: meeting notes, to-discuss pile.

*** Inbox
Unprocessed captured stuff with no decided meaning yet. Lives in =inbox.org=
(desktop/web) and =mobin.org= (mobile); both carry =#+filetags: inbox=.
/Avoid/: todo list, queue.

*** Stuck Project
A =:prj:= with no actionable forward state (=NEXT=/=WAIT=/=TICK=). The weekly
review must give every stuck project a Next Action or park/cancel it.

*** Daily Note
An org-roam note under =notes/daily/= — a log/journal surface only, never a
task bucket. A TODO-state heading found there is a /stray task/ to be swept
out to its real home during review.
/Avoid/: journal (reserved: see below), inbox, capture surface.

*** Stray Task
A TODO-state heading that has leaked into a daily note. The review sweeps these
out; they should never live in =notes/daily/=.
