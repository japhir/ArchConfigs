* daily review of %U [/]
a short end-of-day pass — ~15–20 min. same two phases as the weekly review
(get clear, get current) so the GTD shape becomes muscle memory. the weekly
does the deep work; the daily keeps the system trusted in between.

** get clear [/]
all open loops out of your head and inboxes, into the system.

*** NEXT collect — head, desk, screen
desk / notebook %i, Downloads + Screenshots, anything from today not yet
captured. just capture — don't process yet.
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (dired "~/Downloads")
#+end_src
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (org-timer-set-timer 2)
#+end_src

*** NEXT process every inbox with the decision tree [/]
one pass, every inbox. for each item ask: is it actionable?
- no  → trash, or SOME (someday), or reference (org-roam)
- yes → what is the next action? <2 min: do it now. else delegate → WAIT,
  or defer → SCHEDULE. more than one action → make it a :prj: with an outcome.
(full tree: [[file:weekly_review.org]])
- [ ] inbox.org + mobin.org
- [ ] email — hawaii, solis, gmail
- [ ] Teams / other message streams
- [ ] stray TODO headings in org-roam dailies → refile out
