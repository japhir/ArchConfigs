#+title: 0007 — Line's personal org files live in their own synced folder, outside Ilja's org tree

* Status
accepted

* Decision

Line's own org files (her inbox, todo, etc.) sit in a WebDAV folder of their
own, a sibling of the shared folder, synced to her laptop and phone and simply
not selected for sync on Ilja's machine. They are never a subfolder of
=~/org=.

* Why

Ilja's agenda files are every =.org= under =~/org=, recursively; anything that
lands there is in his agenda. A subfolder with an exclusion rule would work
until the rule is forgotten or the file set changes. A separate folder that is
not synced to his machine cannot leak by construction. It also keeps her
system independent: if the current WebDAV provider (SURFdrive, possibly ending
within months) goes away, her folder moves to any other account as a unit
without disentangling it from his.

Keeping her files only on her phone, as before, was the status quo; it left
them unbacked-up and unreachable from her laptop.

* Consequences

- Her Emacs has two roots, shared and personal; both are agenda files for
  her, only the shared one is for him.
- The shared folder is the sole overlap between the two systems. Anything
  meant for both of us goes there; anything else does not.
- Her personal files carry no assignee tags and plain =DONE=; the who is
  implied by the folder.
